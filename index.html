How the model works

The series studies human ingenuity: businesses and real-world wonders that worked, and worked well, without AI.

1. The Gate (wrapper)
Five tests decide whether a subject is worth 4 to 8 weeks of research. Ingenuity and Evidence are knockouts: a weak score on either stops the subject.
2. The Canvas (the framework)
Four lenses, each asked the same three questions: Constraint, Leap, Consequence. Twelve cells make one comparable page per subject.
3. The Lesson (summary)
An honest ledger of who gained and who paid, where the lenses collide, and one transferable principle for a young person.

Overlap rule: wages as a share of cost is money, so it lives in Economy. Headcount and skills are humans, so they live in People.

Evidence standard

Anchor every key claim in a primary source or two independent credible ones. Make every number traceable. Steel-man the strongest critic. Use explainers for orientation only, never as the sole source.

Series rhythm

One subject, 4 to 8 weeks of research, one flagship episode, plus one or two deep-dive episodes on the strongest lenses.
